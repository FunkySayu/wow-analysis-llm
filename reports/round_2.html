How the spec actually works

Salvo is the engine, and only one payoff is non-linear

Arcane Salvo caps at 25 — 20 from the base talent, +5 from Spellfire Salvo. Confirmed against the logs, where the buff never exceeds 25 stacks.

Generation is concentrated, not spread across the kit. Arcane Missiles is the engine; Blast and Prismatic Bolt are the fine adjustment that bridges the last few stacks.

Missiles is also your main Arcane Charge generator — High Voltage, ~3.5 charges per channel. Not Arcane Blast.

Barrage is the only spender, and it pays out four ways at once

Salvo damage bonus (3%/stack) · Prismatic Bolt proc (2% per stack consumed — 1% base plus 1% from the third apex node, so 50% at cap) · one Meteorite per 5 stacks consumed · Orb Barrage (4% per stack held).

Three of those four are linear in stacks consumed. Dumping at 12 twice yields roughly the same procs as dumping at 25 once. The one genuinely non-linear payoff is Intuition: +25% Barrage damage, only on reaching max stacks. That, plus the fixed GCD cost of every Barrage, is the entire case for holding to 25.

The same arithmetic runs in reverse, and that is round two's finding: a channel is worth ~12 stacks, so starting one above 13 pushes the surplus into the ceiling, where it buys nothing.

# the two legal Barrage triggers, from the sim's own priority list arcane_missiles,if=buff.clearcasting.react&buff.arcane_salvo.stack<12,chain=1 arcane_barrage,if=arcane_charge=4&( (clearcasting&salvo>=12) | salvo=25 ) | buff.arcane_soul.up

The salvo<12 gate on Missiles exists precisely because a channel adds ~12 — starting one above that overflows the cap.

Four things that were already right

Each was checked and cleared. Two looked like problems until they were compared against the sim.

Downtime. Median delay after a Missiles channel is 0.12s. You fire 210 GCDs against the sim's 198. Mechanically you are fast — there is nothing to win here.

Cooldowns. Surge → Touch of the Magi pairing lands within 0.2–0.6s every time, with 4 Surges and 7 Touches per pull. Both ahead of the sim.

Prismatic Bolt. 93% of casts at Cumulative Power 8/8, correct under your 4-piece. Holding the proc around 3 seconds is intended behaviour, not a mistake.

Clearcasting overcap. Nearly flagged — 58–74 wasted procs per pull looks alarming. But the sim has Clearcasting up on 94% of its Barrages too, with a comparable capped rate. Structural oversupply, not your error.

Finding 05 — the new one

Missiles channels started too high

You went from spending too early to filling too far. 32% of Missiles casts now start at Salvo ≥12, up from 18%; 21% start at ≥15. The sim never does.

Where a Missiles channel begins

112 channels across both round-2 pulls. A channel is worth 11.8 stacks, calibrated from your own 66 uncapped channels — so anything begun above 13 pushes the surplus into the ceiling.

Missiles casts — round 2 Overflow zone

~62 Salvo stacks per pull go into the ceiling. At 2% Prismatic Bolt chance per stack consumed and one Meteorite per five, that is roughly 1.2 Prismatic Bolts and 12 Meteorites not fired each pull.

What triggers it

Of the 24 channels started at ≥15, the cast immediately before was Prismatic Bolt in 13 cases — it lands mid-cycle, adds its +4, pushes you from ~13 into the high teens, and the channel goes out anyway — and another Missiles in 10 cases. The signature, from pull 1:

102.5 Missiles salvo 1 106.3 PrismBolt salvo 13 → +4 pushes you to ~18 106.4 Missiles salvo 18 ← channel caps, ~5 stacks binned 108.3 BARRAGE salvo 25

Compare 116.6s in the same pull, which is textbook: Missiles(5) → Blast(16) → Blast(19) → Blast(21) → Blast(23) → Barrage(25). You already do this correctly; it is just inconsistent.

The objection I checked, and it does not hold

The obvious counter is that holding the channel would overcap Clearcasting. It would not: at those 24 casts Clearcasting sat at 1–2 stacks in 20 of 24 cases. Holding was free almost every time.

Read this before comparing damage

Round 2's DPS is not a regression

90.7k and 97.9k against round 1's 134–136k. That is the cooldowns deliberately removed from the test, not the rotation: no Arcane Surge (a 35% spell-damage window, four times, plus the Phoenix and the Arcane Soul window), no Touch of the Magi (6% of damage outright plus its amp), no Time Warp. Mana starvation without Surge's 425% regen costs more on top — pull 2 spent a stretch under 20%.

The two rounds are not comparable on damage and should not be. On the rotational measures they isolate, round 2 is a clear improvement.

What to change next

One rule, replacing the old one

Salvo ≤ 13 → Arcane Missiles Salvo 14–24 → Arcane Blast, or Prismatic Bolt if Cumulative Power is 8 Salvo 25 → Arcane Barrage

13 is the threshold because 13 + 11.8 lands on 25 exactly.

  • After a Prismatic Bolt, re-check Salvo before starting a channel. That single habit covers 13 of the 24 overflow cases.
  • Two Missiles channels per Barrage cycle, then top off with Blast. A third channel in the same cycle is where the rest of the overflow comes from.
  • Keep doing what round 2 fixed. Holding to 25 and leaving Arcane Orb alone are both working — do not trade one back for the other.
