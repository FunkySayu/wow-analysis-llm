How the spec actually works

Salvo is the engine, and only one payoff is non-linear

Arcane Salvo caps at 25 — 20 from the base talent, +5 from Spellfire Salvo. Confirmed against the logs, where the buff never exceeds 25 stacks.

Generation is concentrated, not spread across the kit. Arcane Missiles is the engine; Blast and Prismatic Bolt are the fine adjustment that bridges the last few stacks.

Missiles is also your main Arcane Charge generator — High Voltage, ~3.5 charges per channel. Not Arcane Blast.

Barrage is the only spender, and it pays out four ways at once

Salvo damage bonus (3%/stack) · Prismatic Bolt proc (2% per stack consumed — 1% base plus 1% from the third apex node, so 50% at cap) · one Meteorite per 5 stacks consumed · Orb Barrage (4% per stack held).

Three of those four are linear in stacks consumed. Dumping at 12 twice yields roughly the same procs as dumping at 25 once. The one genuinely non-linear payoff is Intuition: +25% Barrage damage, only on reaching max stacks. That, plus the fixed GCD cost of every Barrage, is the entire case for holding to 25 — and it is exactly what is being left behind.

02

Ten extra Barrages, ten fewer Blastsroot cause

You consume roughly the same total Salvo as the sim — spread across ten more Barrage GCDs. The sim spends those GCDs on Arcane Blast to bridge 21 → 25; you spend them on additional, weaker Barrages. Salvo also sits pinned at the 25 cap for ~22 seconds per pull, and 18% of your Missiles casts start above the <12 gate, so the drift runs in both directions. It is cycle timing, not a one-way bias.

03

Arcane Orb is being hard-cast eight times a pulleasy fix

The sim hard-casts it once. Orb is gated to arcane_charge < 1 — it is a charge fallback, not a damage button, and Orb Barrage already hands you roughly 48 free orbs per pull. That is about seven GCDs per pull on your lowest-value spell. Arcane Blast strictly beats it as a builder: 2 charges, 2 Salvo, and real damage.

04

Arcane Soul windows are entered nearly emptysecondary

The window lands a fixed 17.4 seconds after Arcane Surge — entirely predictable. Barrage does not consume Salvo inside it, so every Barrage fires at whatever you brought in. You entered 7 of 15 windows at 3 Salvo or fewer. Bank to 25 as it approaches. Weighted lower than the items above because the sim is not clean here either.

Do not spend effort here

Four things that are already right

Each of these was checked and cleared. Two of them looked like problems until they were compared against the sim.

Downtime. Median delay after a Missiles channel is 0.12s. You fire 210 GCDs against the sim's 198. Mechanically you are fast — there is nothing to win here.

Cooldowns. Surge → Touch of the Magi pairing lands within 0.2–0.6s every time, with 4 Surges and 7 Touches per pull. Both ahead of the sim.

Prismatic Bolt. 93% of casts at Cumulative Power 8/8, which is correct under your 4-piece. Holding the proc around 3 seconds is the intended behaviour, not a mistake.

Clearcasting overcap. Nearly flagged — 58–74 wasted procs per pull looks alarming. But the sim has Clearcasting up on 94% of its Barrages too, with a comparable capped rate. It is structural oversupply, not your error.

What to change

Three habits, in priority order

  • Count to 25 before Barrage. After a Barrage resets you to 0, it takes two full Missiles channels to reach the cap. If you land at 16–21 with no Clearcasting banked, cast Arcane Blast to top off rather than firing Barrage. This is the whole finding.
  • Stop hard-casting Arcane Orb. Press it only when you are genuinely out of Arcane Charges. Those GCDs belong to Arcane Blast.
  • Bank Salvo into Arcane Soul. It arrives 17.4s after Arcane Surge, every time. Arrive at 25.
